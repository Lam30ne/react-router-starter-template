# Regulate — UX Research Plan

## Study 1: Moderated Cold-Start Tests

**Goal**: Understand first-time user comprehension and behavior without instruction.

**Method**: Moderated usability sessions (remote or in-person), 30 minutes each.

**Participants**: 8-12 adults, mixed demographics, no prior exposure to the app.

**Tasks**:
1. Open the app for the first time (observe onboarding interaction)
2. "Use the app however feels natural" (5 minutes)
3. "Find and change a setting" (any setting)
4. "Start a different kind of session" (test discovery of 10-min, open)

**Metrics**:
- Time to first session start
- Onboarding completion vs. skip rate
- Which pathway chosen (Ambient Rhythm vs. External Focus) and why
- Settings discovery rate
- Unprompted comments about safety information
- Task success rates

**Follow-up**: Semi-structured interview about expectations, understanding of what the app does, and comfort level.

---

## Study 2: Comprehension Check

**Goal**: Verify that users understand what Regulate does (and doesn't claim to do).

**Method**: Survey after 10-minute exposure.

**Participants**: 20-30 adults.

**Questions**:
- "In your own words, what does this app do?"
- "Does this app measure anything about your body?" (expected: no)
- "Would you describe this as a medical tool?" (expected: no)
- "What does 'External Focus' mean to you?"
- "Did you notice any health or safety information?"

**Success criteria**: 80%+ correctly identify it as a relaxation/ambient tool, not a medical device.

---

## Study 3: Two-Week Diary Study

**Goal**: Understand real-world usage patterns and subjective experience over time.

**Method**: Daily diary entries via structured form.

**Participants**: 10-15 adults who complete Study 1.

**Daily questions**:
- Did you use Regulate today? (y/n)
- Which session type? (5-min, 10-min, open)
- Which pathway? (Ambient Rhythm, External Focus)
- How would you describe how you felt before? (free text)
- How would you describe how you felt after? (free text)
- Any discomfort or negative experience? (free text)

**Metrics**:
- Usage frequency and duration trends
- Session type preferences over time
- Pathway preferences and switching behavior
- Adverse experience reports
- Dropout rate and reasons

---

## Ethical Considerations

- No physiological data collected
- Participants must be informed this is a wellness tool, not a medical device
- Adverse experience reports trigger immediate follow-up
- Study protocol should be reviewed by an ethics board if conducted in an institutional context
